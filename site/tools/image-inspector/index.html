      How it works

      Reads the file.
      Not the pixels.

      01

      Every metadata block

      EXIF, maker notes, XMP, IPTC, ICC profiles, Photoshop resources, PNG text, HEIF items and Content Credentials. Unknown fields are listed by ID, not dropped.

      02

      AI provenance

      IPTC digital source type, Content Credentials, AI labels, and prompts saved by Stable Diffusion tools, ComfyUI, InvokeAI and Fooocus.

      03

      Privacy exposure

      GPS location, serial numbers, names, embedded thumbnails, hidden depth maps and extra images, Photoshop layers, file paths and SVG scripts.

      Honest limits

      A clue is not a verdict.

      No tool can prove from metadata alone whether an image was made by AI.

      01

      Missing metadata proves nothing

      Social platforms, messaging apps and screenshots usually strip it. A clean result does not mean the image is human-made.

      02

      Metadata can be edited

      Anyone can add, remove or change these fields. Declarations are reported as statements, not proof.

      03

      Some data is listed, not decoded

      Vendor maker notes show identifying fields by name and the rest by tag ID. Brotli-compressed JPEG XL boxes are listed without their contents. Content Credentials signatures are not verified, and invisible watermarks such as SynthID cannot be detected.

      04

      Verify it yourself

      Once the page has loaded, disconnect from the internet and inspect a file. It still works, because nothing leaves your device.
